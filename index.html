SIGNAL RECON

A retro handheld scanner for iPhone and Mac. Track a Bluetooth device on a radar, watch your Wi-Fi link in real time and list every signal around you.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. We answer within a few days.

FAQ

  • Why is the distance approximate? It is estimated from Bluetooth signal strength, which walls, bodies and pockets change. Stand 1 m from the device and press CAL 1M for better accuracy.
  • Why can't the iPhone list nearby Wi-Fi networks? iOS does not give apps access to Wi-Fi scan results. The Mac version shows nearby networks, channels and the 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz bands.
  • How do I see friends on the radar? Everyone who has Signal Recon open broadcasts a callsign. Other copies show them as squad members.
