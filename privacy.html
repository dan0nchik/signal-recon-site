PRIVACY POLICY

Signal Recon, effective October 10, 2026

Summary

Signal Recon does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything it measures stays on your device.

What the app uses and why

  • Bluetooth: to scan nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices and show their signal strength and estimated distance, and to broadcast a random callsign so other copies of the app nearby can see you. The callsign is not linked to your identity.
  • Location: Apple requires location permission before an app may read Wi-Fi network names. Signal Recon never reads or stores your location.
  • Local network: to ping your router and measure Wi-Fi latency, jitter and packet loss.
  • Camera (optional, iPhone): to show the world behind the on-screen scanner. Video is displayed live and is never recorded or transmitted.

Data sharing

No data is sent to the developer or to third parties. The app makes no network requests except pings to your own router or, if the router does not answer, to the public DNS resolver 1.1.1.1.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
